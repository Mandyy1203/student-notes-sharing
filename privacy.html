Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account, we store your name, email address, and account role via Supabase Authentication to manage your uploads and session security.

2. Uploaded Study Materials

PDF notes, titles, subjects, and branch details you upload are visible to other logged-in students on the platform. Files are stored in a private bucket and accessed through temporary 60-second signed URLs.

3. Analytics & Cookies

We use Google Analytics to understand basic page traffic and essential browser storage to keep you logged in. We never sell or share your personal data with third parties.

4. Account & Content Deletion

You can delete any of your uploaded notes directly from the note details page at any time, which permanently removes both the database record and the PDF file.
